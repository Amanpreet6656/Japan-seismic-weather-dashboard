Emergency Standard Operating Procedures JMA & Cabinet Office

The Japan Meteorological Agency classifies tropical cyclones reaching 10-minute maximum sustained winds of 34 knots (17.2 m/s) as Typhoons. Severity is categorized by central atmospheric pressure and maximum sustained velocity:

JMA Classification Max Sustained Winds Typical Barometric Pressure Immediate Structural Action
Typhoon (Standard) 17.2 - 32.6 m/s (34 - 63 kt) 980 - 1000 hPa Clear balconies, secure shutters, check drain grates.
Very Strong (非常に強い) 32.7 - 43.6 m/s (64 - 84 kt) 940 - 975 hPa Move indoors, avoid transport terminals, prep power bank.
Violent (猛烈な) ≥ 43.7 m/s (≥ 85 kt) < 935 hPa Refrain from outdoor exposure, danger of flying debris.

Municipal disaster authorities issue unified warning levels (Keikai Reberu) requiring graduated public compliance:

Level Official Advisory Title Mandated Public Action
Level 1 Early Warning Information Heighten disaster awareness and monitor meteorological broadcasts.
Level 2 Heavy Rain / Flood Advisory Review municipal hazard maps and confirm family evacuation rendezvous.
Level 3 Elderly / Vulnerable Evacuation Elderly, disabled, and persons requiring support evacuate to shelter.
Level 4 Evacuation Order (避難指示) All residents in hazard zones must evacuate to designated shelters.
Level 5 Emergency Safety Assurance Disaster has occurred. Secure life immediately on upper floors or safe rooms.

Unlike magnitude (which measures seismic energy at the hypocenter), JMA Shindo (震度) quantifies localized ground acceleration experienced at surface observation stations:

  • Shindo 1-3: Perceptible to seated persons. Suspended fixtures sway gently. No structural damage.
  • Shindo 4: Noticeable walking difficulty. Unsecured ornaments may tumble. Slight structural squeaking.
  • Shindo 5-Lower / Upper: Difficult to stand without support. Dishes and book stacks fall. Unreinforced concrete walls crack.
  • Shindo 6-Lower / Upper: Impossible to remain standing. Severe structural shear in older wooden houses. Large wall cracks.
  • Shindo 7: Flung by intense ground acceleration. Reinforced concrete buildings may lean or collapse. Ground fissures appear.

Tsunami waves travel at jetliner velocities across deep oceanic trenches (v = √(g·h)) and slow down near shorelines while swelling dramatically in height:

  • Major Tsunami Warning (Expected height > 3 meters): Immediate vertical evacuation to elevated concrete structures (3rd floor or higher) or inland hills. Do not utilize motor vehicles due to gridlock risk.
  • Tsunami Warning (Expected height 1 - 3 meters): Evacuate coastal regions and rivermouth zones immediately.
  • Tsunami Advisory (Expected height 0.2 - 1 meter): Clear beaches and harbor edges. Marine currents are dangerously violent.
